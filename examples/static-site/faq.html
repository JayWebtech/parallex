FAQ

Does this site set cookies?

No. The Parallax tracker stores nothing in your browser and needs no consent banner.

What does Parallax learn about me?

The page you viewed, the referrer, your country, and your browser, OS and device type. Not your IP address.

I use Do Not Track.

Then this visit is not counted: the snippet sets data-do-not-track="true".
